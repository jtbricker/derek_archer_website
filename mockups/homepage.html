Research Focus

Areas of Investigation

Bridging neuroimaging and genomics to understand brain aging and neurodegeneration at scale.

🧠

Neuroimaging Biomarkers

Identifying in-vivo structural brain changes associated with neurodegeneration using diffusion MRI and advanced imaging techniques.

🧬

Computational Genetics

Leveraging genome-wide association studies and polygenic risk scores to map genetic susceptibility to brain structural changes.

🔌

White Matter Neurodegeneration

Investigating how vascular risk, genetics, and AD pathology drive white matter decline, with multi-site MRI harmonization for large-scale studies.

💻

AI-Driven Brain Signatures

Applying deep learning to large-scale imaging and genetics datasets to build predictive brain age and disease signatures.

# Midnight Lab

> A neuroscience lab at night — screens glowing with data, the hum of computation.

![Midnight Lab wireframe](midnight-lab.svg)

---

## Vibe
Dark, immersive, data-forward. The kind of site a researcher builds when their work *is* the aesthetic. Think: terminal windows, fMRI scans at 2am, the glow of a monitor in a dark office. Serious but alive with quiet energy.

## Typography
- **Headings:** Inter — geometric, clean, authoritative at large sizes
- **Body:** Source Sans 3 — humanist, excellent readability for academic prose
- **Code/Data:** JetBrains Mono — DOIs, PMIDs, technical labels

## Page Elements

### Header
- Fixed, semi-transparent with backdrop blur
- Name on left, nav links on right
- Active page highlighted in teal
- Collapses to hamburger on mobile

### Hero Section
- Full-viewport height, left-aligned text
- Subtle grid pattern in background (suggests data, computation)
- Soft radial glow orbs in teal and purple (ambient, not distracting)
- Monospace eyebrow label: "NEUROIMAGING & COMPUTATIONAL GENETICS"
- Name in large display type with gradient accent on ", PhD"
- 2-line research summary in muted text
- Affiliation with gold highlight on "Vanderbilt University Medical Center"
- Two CTA buttons: filled teal "Explore Research" and outlined "Publications"

### Metrics Banner
- Horizontal strip below hero, darker surface
- Four key numbers in large teal type: citations, paper count, cohorts, award
- Small labels beneath each in muted text
- Subtle glow effect on numbers

### Research Areas
- Section eyebrow in monospace teal
- Four glass-morphism cards in a row
- Each card: colored icon (emoji in tinted square), title, 2-line description
- Cards have subtle border, lift on hover
- Color-coded by theme: teal for imaging, purple for genetics, gold for AI

### Publications
- Left-border accent lines (teal for highlighted, muted for others)
- Each entry: bold title, author line with "Archer DB" emphasized, journal in teal, year
- Small pill badges: "1st Author" (teal) or "Last Author" (gold)
- "View All" button centered below

### Collaborations
- Four cards in a row, centered text
- Large gold acronym (VMAP, VADRC, CNT, VALIANT)
- Full name below in muted text
- Role in monospace at bottom

### Footer
- Three-column: brand info, page links, external links
- Copyright centered below a faint divider

## What Makes This Design Distinctive
- **Dark-mode-native** — no light theme, this is the theme. Feels like you're looking at a research dashboard.
- **Glass-morphism cards** — semi-transparent backgrounds with blur, giving depth without heavy shadows
- **Teal glow** — key numbers and interactive elements have a soft luminous quality
- **Grid background** — subtle nod to data/computation without being literal
- **Monospace labels** — the eyebrow text feels like terminal output, connecting to the computational aspect
- **Restrained color** — mostly navy and slate, with teal/gold/purple used sparingly for maximum impact

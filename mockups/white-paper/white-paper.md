# White Paper

> An elegantly typeset journal article — the research speaks for itself.

![White Paper wireframe](white-paper.svg)

---

## Vibe
Clean, restrained, typographically rich. The academic equivalent of a well-designed journal layout. White space is a feature, not an absence. Serif headings give gravitas; the content doesn't need visual tricks to be compelling. Think: a beautifully typeset CV you'd print for a tenure committee, but alive on screen.

## Typography
- **Headings:** Georgia (serif) — classic, authoritative, academic tradition
- **Body/Labels:** Inter — clean sans-serif for navigation, metadata, UI elements
- **Emphasis:** Georgia italic for the research summary — gives it the feel of an abstract

## Page Elements

### Header
- Clean white bar, thin bottom border
- Name in Georgia serif on the left, "PhD" in italic
- Nav links in Inter sans-serif on the right
- Active page underlined in blue
- Minimal, no blur effects or transparency

### Hero Section
- White background, generous whitespace
- Thin blue gradient bar at the very top (the only decorative element)
- Name in large Georgia serif
- Title in spaced uppercase sans-serif
- Affiliation on one line with bullet separator
- Short horizontal rule as visual divider
- Research summary in Georgia italic — reads like an abstract
- Headshot placeholder on the right (rounded rectangle, neutral)
- Two buttons: filled blue "View CV" and outlined "Google Scholar"

### Metrics Strip
- Subtle gray background, thin borders top and bottom
- Four numbers separated by vertical hairlines
- Numbers in Georgia serif (large), labels in Inter (small)
- No color on the numbers — pure black-on-gray

### Research Areas
- 2x2 grid of white cards with subtle border
- Each card has a thin colored left border (4px) — each area gets its own color
- Title in Inter bold, description in Georgia
- No icons — the colored bar is the only visual differentiation
- Cards are clean, flat, no shadows or hover effects described

### Publications
- Pure typographic list — no cards, no colored backgrounds
- Title in Georgia bold, metadata in Inter
- Journal name highlighted in blue
- Thin hairline separator between entries
- This is the most "journal-like" section — intentionally sparse

### Projects
- Simple cards on gray background
- Acronym in Georgia bold, full name in Inter small
- Minimal, functional

### Footer
- Light gray bar
- Name, affiliation, email on one line
- Copyright right-aligned
- Single line — no multi-column layout

## What Makes This Design Distinctive
- **Serif-forward** — Georgia for all headings and content text. This is unusual for a tech/science site and immediately says "academic, serious, established."
- **No dark mode** — This design is unapologetically light. It trusts the content.
- **Colored left borders** instead of icons — research areas are distinguished by a slim vertical bar, not emoji or SVG icons. Cleaner and more grown-up.
- **Journal-style publications** — The publication list looks like a bibliography, not a dashboard. Titles in serif, journals in blue. No badges.
- **Generous whitespace** — The page breathes. Nothing is crowded. Every element has room.
- **Italic abstract** — The research summary in the hero is set in italic Georgia, making it feel like the opening of a paper.
- **Headshot included** — This concept is the only one that includes a photo area, reinforcing the personal/professional nature.
